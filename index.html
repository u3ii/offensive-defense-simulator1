Project Idea

The Offensive-Informed Defense Simulator is a cybersecurity simulation platform designed to analyze system security by modeling attacker behavior within a controlled environment. The project simulates the stages of a cyber attack lifecycle such as reconnaissance, initial access, privilege escalation, and access to sensitive assets. Instead of performing real attacks, the system analyzes how an attacker could progress through different attack paths and evaluates where defenses succeed or fail. The platform also integrates an AI-based decision engine that analyzes system conditions and dynamically selects possible attack paths, allowing the simulation to mimic more realistic attacker behavior and provide insights for improving defensive strategies.
